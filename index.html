About Me

A data analyst with real domain roots — from hospital records to my own farmland.

My path into data is a little different — and that's my edge. I hold an MBA in Agribusiness Management and managed real operational data, from hospital diagnostic records to a hydroponic farm project, before formally training as an analyst through the PwC-designed, NSDC-certified Data Analytics with AI program.

Long-term, I'm working toward a 2-acre model farm project in Itki, Jharkhand — combining hands-on agriculture with the data tools I'm building now. That's why my flagship personal project is a farmer advisory app: I build for problems I've seen firsthand.

Beyond the core analytics stack, I build with AI — including Jarvis, a multi-agent system on Claude Code — to automate the repetitive parts of analysis and focus on the insight that matters.
